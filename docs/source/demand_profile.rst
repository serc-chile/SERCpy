Demand Profiles
===============

Introduction
------------

Solar energy is intermittent by nature. For this reason, being able to estimate the power demanded by a thermal load with a high time-resolution is a crucial step in the development of an accurate simulation of a solar thermal energy system.

Another element that greatly impacts the performance of solar thermal systems is the fact that thermal loads tend to demand more energy during time periods when solar energy availability is lower, such as winter, and on days with lower temperature in general.

:doc:`DemandProfile <demand_profile_class>` is a class provided by **SERCpy** that seeks to address both of these issues. It offers the users several construction so that they can choose the one that best matches their case and the data they have access to.
